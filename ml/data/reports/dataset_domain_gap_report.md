# Nutrition AI – Dataset Domain Gap & Real-World Audit Report

**Date:** November 3, 2026  
**Author:** Senior Machine Learning Engineer & Computer Vision Researcher  
**Status:** Audit Complete & Upgrade Roadmap Established

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Analysis

The Nutrition AI 650-class model demonstrated strong performance on its internal validation split (**81.85% Top-1 / 96.42% Top-5 accuracy**), but exhibited significant degradation when tested on uncontrolled real-world smartphone and web photographs (**0.0% – 4.0% Top-1 accuracy**).

This report investigates the architectural and data-level causes of this performance divergence and defines the comprehensive real-world upgrade roadmap.

---

## 2. Dataset Pipeline Audit

### 2.1 Current Composition & Image Sourcing
* **Total Image Count:** 195,000 images across 650 food classes.
* **Class Distribution:** Uniform distribution of exactly 300 images per class.
* **Sourcing Breakdown:**
  * **Procedural Synthetic Images:** **194,995 images (99.997%)** generated by `dataset_generator_650.py` using Pillow 2D geometric ellipses and RGB noise.
  * **Authentic Real Photographs:** **5 images (0.003%)**.

### 2.2 Preprocessing & Augmentation Strategy
* **Pre-training Preprocessing:** Resized to $224 \times 224$ and normalized to ImageNet statistics ($\mu=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406]$, $\sigma=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]$).
* **Historical Augmentations:** Fixed in-memory tensor transformations without on-the-fly photographic perturbations (no camera lens distortion, no specular reflection modeling, no dynamic lighting adjustments).

## 3. Real-World Failure Examples

### Case Study 1: Real Rainbow Salad Bowl
* **Input Image:** High-resolution mobile photograph of a mixed vegetable salad bowl (lettuce, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, shredded carrots, bell peppers, blueberries).
* **Model Output:** `Bratwurst Veal Cooked` (Confidence: **3.29%**, `status: "unsure"`).
* **Root Cause:**
  1. No authentic salad textures in training data (only solid colored ellipses).
  2. Softmax activation was completely dispersed across all 650 classes, with the highest logit receiving only 3.29%.
  3. The system appropriately marked the prediction as uncertain, but failed to recognize the dish.

### Case Study 2: Real Gala Apple
* **Input Image:** Web photograph of a fresh Gala apple on a clean kitchen counter.
* **Model Output:** `Burdock Root Raw` (Confidence: **6.17%**, `status: "unsure"`).
* **Root Cause:**
  1. Apple class was trained on flat red circular graphics without specular highlights, natural stem indentations, or fruit skin mottling.
  2. Pretrained backbone features could not match the geometric drawings to the photographic input.

---

## 4. Key Limitations of the Synthetic Dataset

1. **Absence of Photographic Noise & Camera Optics:**
   Real phone cameras produce lens blur, bokeh, sensor ISO grain, white balance shifts, and specular glare on oily or wet foods. Procedural Pillow graphics lack these high-frequency optical signals.

2. **Single-Object Bias vs. Mixed-Plate Realities:**
   Procedural generators render a single centered shape. Real meals (salads, thalis, combo bowls) contain multiple overlapping ingredients and complex geometries.

3. **Heavy-Tail Frequency Mismatch:**
   In the real world, 20% of food items (apples, bananas, rice, chicken, salad, pizza, eggs) account for 80% of user uploads. The synthetic dataset treated rare ingredients (e.g., `abiyuch_raw`, `balsam_pear`) identically to daily staples.

---

## 5. Upgrade Action Plan

To bridge the domain gap and achieve $\ge 75\%$ real-world Top-1 accuracy:
1. **Real-World Dataset Ingestion:** Ingest real photographic benchmarks (Food-101, UEC Food, Open Food Facts, Indian Food Dataset).
2. **High-Fidelity Photographic Synthesis:** Upgrade synthetic generators with realistic food textures, organic edge perturbations, specular noise, and complex multi-layer compositing.
3. **Class Balancing & Weighted Sampling:** Scale staple classes to 1,000 samples and secondary classes to 500 samples.
4. **Photographic Augmentation Engine:** Train with RandAugment, ColorJitter, RandomResizedCrop, MixUp, and CutMix.
5. **Real-World Validation Benchmark:** Validate on 1,000+ real-world mobile camera photos across 6 core food domains.
